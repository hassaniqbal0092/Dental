Our Dental Services

Comprehensive care for every stage of life — click any service to see what's involved and how long it takes.

General Checkups & Cleanings

Routine exams, professional cleaning, and cavity screening to keep your smile healthy.

Book Now

What it involves

Comprehensive oral exam, digital X-rays as needed, professional plaque & tartar removal, polishing, and personalized hygiene guidance.

Typical duration

45–60 minutes. Recommended every 6 months.

Teeth Whitening

Professional-grade whitening for a noticeably brighter smile in one visit.

Book Now

What it involves

In-office bleaching treatment using a high-concentration whitening gel activated with light, safely brightening teeth several shades.

Typical duration

60–90 minutes for a single in-office session.

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-looking tooth replacement backed by advanced technology.

Book Now

What it involves

A titanium post is placed in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth root, later topped with a custom crown for a natural look and feel.

Typical duration

Placement takes 1–2 hours; full treatment spans 3–6 months including healing.

Root Canal Therapy

Relieve pain and save an infected tooth with gentle, modern root canal treatment.

Book Now

What it involves

Removal of infected or inflamed pulp from inside the tooth, thorough cleaning and disinfection, then sealing to prevent further infection.

Typical duration

60–90 minutes, sometimes split across two visits.

Orthodontics / Invisalign

Straighten your smile discreetly with clear aligners or traditional braces.

Book Now

What it involves

Custom treatment plan using clear Invisalign aligners or traditional braces to gradually straighten teeth and correct bite issues.

Typical duration

Treatment ranges 6–24 months depending on complexity; check-ins every 6–8 weeks.

Crowns & Bridges

Restore damaged teeth or replace missing ones with durable, custom-fitted restorations.

Book Now

What it involves

Custom-made caps (crowns) or connected replacement teeth (bridges), crafted from impressions and bonded in place for a natural fit.

Typical duration

Two visits, about 2 weeks apart, roughly 60–90 minutes each.

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle, kid-friendly dental care that builds healthy habits early.

Book Now

What it involves

Friendly checkups, cleanings, fluoride treatments, and sealants tailored to children, delivered in a fun, low-stress environment.

Typical duration

30–45 minutes. Recommended every 6 months starting at age 1.

Emergency Dental Care

Same-day appointments for urgent dental pain, injuries, and repairs.

Book Now

What it involves

Rapid evaluation and treatment for toothaches, broken teeth, lost fillings/crowns, and dental trauma.

Typical duration

Varies by case; same-day visits typically available within a few hours.

Cosmetic Dentistry / Veneers

Transform your smile's shape, color, and alignment with custom veneers.

Book Now

What it involves

Thin, custom-shaded porcelain shells bonded to the front of teeth to improve shape, color, and alignment for a flawless smile.

Typical duration

Two visits, roughly 2 weeks apart, about 60–90 minutes each.

Tooth Extractions

Safe, comfortable removal of damaged or problematic teeth when needed.

Book Now

What it involves

Gentle local anesthesia followed by careful removal of the tooth, with aftercare guidance to support fast, comfortable healing.

Typical duration

20–40 minutes for routine extractions; longer for surgical cases.
