Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit. Can't find your answer? Contact us.

Insurance & Payment Plans

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes — we accept most major dental insurance plans and will help you understand your coverage before treatment. Our front desk team can verify your benefits ahead of your appointment.

Do you offer payment plans or financing?

Yes. We offer flexible in-house payment plans and partner with third-party financing providers for larger treatments like implants, orthodontics, or veneers.

What if I don't have insurance?

No problem — we offer a discounted in-house membership plan that covers routine checkups, cleanings, and reduced rates on other treatments.

Your First Visit

What should I expect at my first appointment?

Your first visit includes a full oral exam, digital X-rays if needed, a professional cleaning, and time to discuss any concerns or treatment goals with your dentist.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card (if applicable), and a list of any current medications. Arriving 15 minutes early to complete paperwork is recommended.

How long does a first appointment take?

Plan for approximately 60–75 minutes for a comprehensive first visit, including exam, X-rays, and cleaning.

Emergency Contact

What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

Call our office immediately at (555) 123-4567. We reserve same-day slots for urgent issues like severe pain, swelling, or a knocked-out tooth.

Do you offer emergency care after hours?

Our answering service can reach the on-call dentist for true emergencies outside office hours. Follow the voicemail prompts for urgent assistance.

Sedation Options

Do you offer sedation for anxious patients?

Yes — we offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and oral conscious sedation for patients with dental anxiety or those undergoing longer procedures. We'll discuss the best option for your comfort level.

Is sedation safe for children?

Mild sedation options like nitrous oxide are safe and commonly used for children when appropriate, always under careful monitoring by our pediatric-trained team.

Cancellation Policy

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or missed appointments may incur a small fee.

How do I reschedule my appointment?

Simply call our office or use the booking form on our appointments page to request a new time.

Children's Dentistry

At what age should my child first visit the dentist?

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends a first visit by age 1, or within six months of the first tooth appearing.

Do you have a dentist who specializes in children?

Yes — Dr. Priya Nair is our dedicated pediatric dentist, and our whole team is trained to make visits comfortable and fun for kids of all ages.

Can parents stay in the room during my child's appointment?

Absolutely. Parents and guardians are always welcome to stay with their child throughout the visit.

Still have questions?

Our friendly team is happy to help — reach out anytime.
